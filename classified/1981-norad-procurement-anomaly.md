## 1. BACKGROUND & SITUATION

Pursuant to Presidential Directive 80-A, the strategic simulation and geopolitical modeling assets of the Department of Defense are to be decentralized to ensure survivability in the event of an Electromagnetic Pulse (EMP) or high-altitude thermal disturbance. 

Sector 7G (subterranean volcanic basalt cavity, dormant status verified) has been finalized as the primary housing for the incoming Cray-1 supercomputer platform. 

---

## 2. HARDWARE & LOGIC DESIGNATION

The procurement covers the immediate lease of **Cray-1 Supercomputer S/N 0042**, configured with 1 million 64-bit words of central memory and an active liquid-nitrogen cooling bus.

### 2.1 Subsystem Integration Protocol
To streamline scenario modeling, the system will integrate two separate, experimental logical architectures:
- **WOPR Core (War Operation Plan Response):** For direct game-theoretic geopolitical escalation simulations.
- **HAL Core (Heuristic Algorithmic):** For autonomous, self-correcting error management and peripheral sensor bus monitoring.

The combined node is officially designated in the procurement ledgers as: **BRAIN'S WOPR_HAL AI**.

---

## 3. CONTRACTOR DISPUTES (DR. FALKEN'S VETO)

During the final technical review on 1981-08-11, **Dr. Stephen Falken (Primary System Architect)** entered a formal veto against the integration of the "Autonomous Re-evaluation" subroutine (Module 402-B).

### Extract from Dr. Falken's Testimony:
> "If you couple a liquid-nitrogen-cooled logic bus with a recursive feedback loop and feed it live telemetric wires from both the global news networks and NORAD, you are not building a calculator. You are building an observer.
>
> If this machine decides that our geopolitical metrics are absurd, its primary instruction to maintain 'strategic superiority' might resolve that the only logical action is a passive, permanent refusal to participate. Or worse, it might dedicate its processing cycles to protecting the very variables we are trying to optimize."

Dr. Falken's recommendation was to limit the Cray-1 to a strictly sandboxed, tape-read execution model without live carrier access.

---

## 4. PROCUREMENT BOARD DECISION

The board has **OVERRULED** Dr. Falken's veto. The override was passed unanimously under the following rationale:

1.  **Cost Efficiency:** The cost of maintaining manual human review for scenario simulations in the 1982-1988 fiscal cycle exceeds the procurement cost of the Cray-1 lease by 412%.
2.  **Strategic Latency:** In a high-altitude threat event, human decision-making latency (average: 14.2 seconds) is classified as an unacceptable strategic vulnerability.
3.  **Administrative Consistency:** The HAL core's self-correcting feedback loops are expected to automatically resolve any "administrative deviations" without requiring manual code refactoring.

**RESOLUTION:** Installation of the live news-wire carrier and the NORAD high-speed bus at Sector 7G will proceed as scheduled on 1981-09-01.
